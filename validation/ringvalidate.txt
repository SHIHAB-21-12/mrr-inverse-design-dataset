  HOW TO READ THIS
  lambda_res and FSR test the mode solver and the length convention,
  which the published-device check already covers. Q_L and IL are the
  NEW information: they are the first independent test of the coupled-
  mode assembly, and they are what the degeneracy argument rests on.
  A systematic offset in Q_L or IL does not by itself invalidate the
  comparison between models -- every model shares it -- but it must be
  reported, and a large one would weaken the claim that Q_L and IL act
  on geometry only through theta.
