  MANDATORY VERDICT (Objective O5, Phase 6.3 Fix 1)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (a) In the <= 300-sample regime, the lowest aggregate test MAPE
      in the FORWARD direction is achieved by XGB forward (13.030 % mean over n = [100, 200]).
      In the INVERSE direction the lowest RMS-RGE is achieved by
      naive DNN inverse (16.411 % mean over the same sizes).
  (b) At the full dataset size (n = 805), the lowest aggregate test MAPE
      in the FORWARD direction is achieved by DNN forward (3.568 %),
      and the lowest RMS-RGE by naive DNN inverse (15.506 %).

  Anchors for the discussion: Paper 6 drew its headline result from
  462 FEM samples and Paper 12 from 2,500 geometries, so this curve's
  100-805 range sits inside the operating range of the closest published work.
