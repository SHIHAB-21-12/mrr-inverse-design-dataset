PHASE 7 -- APPLICATION-SPECIFIC VALIDATION (CPO MAPPING)
======================================================================================
Target tuples are CONSTRUCTED here, because Phase 7.3 states each
spec as a band or a bound while Phase 7.4 step 1 needs a point target.
Rule used: a bound is targeted at its stated value, a band at its
geometric mean, and IL at half its limit so the query asks for margin
rather than for a design sitting exactly at the failure edge.
A different construction would move these numbers; state the rule in the
thesis alongside the results.

7.5  THERMAL TUNABILITY (qualitative, out of core scope)
======================================================================================
  at n_g = 4.2: dlambda/dT ~ 1550/4.2 x 1.86e-04 = 68.6 pm/K
  at n_g = 4.31: dlambda/dT ~ 1550/4.31 x 1.86e-04 = 66.9 pm/K
  v1.3 note: Eq. 19 needs the MODAL dn_eff/dT, while 1.86e-4 is the MATERIAL
  dn/dT for silicon; the two coincide only at unity confinement, so the
  quoted 70-90 pm/K band is right at its lower end and optimistic at its upper.
  A fabrication offset of one channel spacing would need:
    Scenario A: 4 nm / 68.6 pm/K = 58 K of tuning
    Scenario B: 0.8 nm / 68.6 pm/K = 12 K of tuning
  Use the mode solver's own dn_eff/dT if this extension is pursued.
