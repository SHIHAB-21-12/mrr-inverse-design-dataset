PHASE 1.6 SUB-STUDY 2 - MESH CONVERGENCE REPORT
====================================================================
Requirement: sweep mesh resolution on one representative geometry and
confirm the transmittance gradient falls below ~1e-4 (method modelled on
Paper 10, Section 2.3).
Representative geometry: R = 10 um, w = 450 nm, g = 200 nm, Lc = 1.5 um
Sweep variable: scalar mesh override d (dx = dy = dz = d) over both
coupling regions, global mesh accuracy held at 3.

THREE SWEEPS WERE RUN. ONLY THE THIRD IS USED.
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  File: DEFECTIVE_sweep_50ps_2001pts.csv       STATUS: INVALID
  Three defects were found in it and corrected in the code:
   1. Spectral grid 0.050 nm against a true linewidth of 0.029 nm. The
      Section 4.1.4 item 3 check ("verify against the narrowest expected
      linewidth") failed. Raised to 20,001 points (0.005 nm).

FINDING: THE 1e-4 CRITERION IS NOT MET IN A MEANINGFUL SENSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The script reported "converged at d = 8 nm" because the final pair of
points happened to land close together. That verdict is not valid: the
sequence is non-monotonic (falls for four points, then rises), so a
gradient test on adjacent pairs cannot establish convergence. kappa^2
instead scatters by about +/-2.5% around 0.0754 across a 4x refinement.
The probable cause is staircasing: the bends are curved and the mesh is
rectangular, so each mesh size discretises the curve slightly
differently, shifting the coupling by an amount that does not shrink
with refinement.

DECISION: PRODUCTION MESH d = 32 nm
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - d = 32 nm is the only point inside the 16.8 min/sample budget
    established by sub-study 1 (N = 800 in the four-week window).
  - Refining 4x changes kappa^2 by at most ~2.5% while costing 12x in
    runtime; the coarse mesh sits inside the scatter band.
  - kappa^2 is therefore reported as 0.0754 +/- 2.5% at this geometry,
    and this scatter is carried as a stated uncertainty rather than
    claimed as convergence.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The 5-parameter kappa^2 model reaches 3.37% cross-validated MAPE, of
which ~2.5% is this mesh-scatter floor: 20 of the 48 held-out errors lie
inside +/-2.5%. The model is at the limit the measurements can support.
