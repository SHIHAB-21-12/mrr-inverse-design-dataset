VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Both solver paths exceed the 25 min/sample trigger:

  3-D : 2,100 min/sample  = 84x the trigger, 140x the 15 min target
  2-D :   148.8 min/sample =  6.0x the trigger, 9.9x the 15 min target

Required to reach N = 800 within the 4-week window at ~8 unattended
batch hours/day: 16.8 min/sample. Neither path is within an order of
magnitude of this.

Continuous compute implied by the 2-D figure:
  N =   800 -> 82.8 days
  N = 1,000 -> 103.5 days

=> The Section 7 fallback is triggered on timing grounds alone.

SECOND, INDEPENDENT GROUND: 2-D VALIDITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2-D FDTD has no vertical radiation loss and overstates Q accordingly:

  Q_L (2-D, measured from linewidth)      : 53,917
  Q_L (3-D, estimated from log decay)     :  4,753
  Ratio                                   : 11.3x

A 2-D bulk dataset would therefore train the ML models on a quality
factor roughly an order of magnitude from the 3-D truth. Section 7
requires this offset be quantified with a 100-150 sample 3-D subset,
but at this magnitude it is a dominant correction, not a small one.
This is a validity objection to 2-D for this device, independent of
runtime.

SUPPORTING RESULT: THE DUAL-FIDELITY PATH IS VIABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
From the single calibrated 2-D spectrum, Eqs. 3, 4 and 6 were inverted
to recover the underlying coupled-mode parameters:

  n_g      = 3.9457      (Eq. 4)
  t1*t2*a  = 0.990256    (Eq. 6 inverted)
  t        = 0.995674    (Eq. 3 at resonance, t1 = t2)
  a        = 0.998879
  kappa^2  = 0.008633
  alpha    = 1.480 dB/cm

Paper 11 (Tang et al. 2024) reports 1.3 dB/cm measured on a fabricated
440 x 220 nm SOI TE ring. Agreement to 0.18 dB/cm, from three mutually
independent equations, indicates the extraction is sound.

Because a coupler-only simulation contains no closed loop, its cost
does not scale with Q - which is precisely why the dual-fidelity route
escapes the constraint that defeats both full-ring paths.

DEFECTS FOUND AND CORRECTED DURING THIS SUB-STUDY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Spectral grid 2,001 points (0.050 nm) against a true 0.029 nm
   linewidth. The Section 4.1.4 item 3 verification clause failed.
   Raised to 20,001 points (0.005 nm).
b. Resonance selection by "nearest 1550 nm" hopped between adjacent
   modes 9.2 nm apart, because 1550 sits at their midpoint. Sub-study
   sweeps now track a fixed mode.
c. Peak position snapped to the DFT grid. Parabolic sub-grid
   interpolation added.

Impact of (a)-(c) on the same geometry:
  T_peak  0.14083 -> 0.78407   IL 8.51 dB -> 1.06 dB
  FWHM    0.14498 -> 0.02866   Q_L 10,660 -> 53,917

The first sweep (DEFECTIVE_sweep_50ps_2001pts.csv) is retained as
evidence of why these corrections were required.
