VALIDATION OF THE DUAL-FIDELITY ANALYTIC METHOD
====================================================================
Question: does feeding coupler-simulation inputs through Eqs. 2-7
reproduce what a full-ring simulation actually produces?

TEST DESIGN (closed loop, identical physics on both sides)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Truth  : 2-D FULL-RING FDTD, 600 ps window, 20,001 points, d = 32 nm,
           auto-shutoff reached 9.95e-6 (field fully decayed)
           File: calibration_evidence_600ps.log
  Input  : kappa^2 from the ISOLATED 2-D coupler, same mesh (d = 32 nm)
           File: substudy2_coupler_convergence_2d.csv
  Geometry R = 10 um, w = 450 nm, g = 200 nm, Lc = 1.5 um
  Both sides are 2-D, so any mismatch isolates error in the analytic
  method itself rather than a 2-D/3-D difference.

(A) INVERSE CONSISTENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  kappa^2 measured on the isolated coupler           : 0.008480
  kappa^2 recovered by inverting the full-ring
  spectrum through Eqs. 3, 4 and 6                   : 0.008633
  Agreement                                          : 1.77%
  (inside the +/-2.5% mesh scatter of sub-study 2)

(B) FORWARD PREDICTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
                   predicted    measured    error
  Q_L              54,788       53,923      1.60%
  T_peak           0.78082      0.78407     0.41%
  IL               1.075 dB     1.057 dB    0.02 dB

LIMIT OF THIS TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
In (B), a and n_g are taken from the same full-ring spectrum, so this
validates the kappa^2 -> response link, which is the core of the method,
but not those two inputs. They are validated independently:
  n_g : sub-study 3, against a fabricated device (Paper 11)
  a   : calibration-derived 1.48 dB/cm vs Paper 11's measured 1.3 dB/cm
Three separate checks, each covering what the others cannot.

PHYSICAL CONSISTENCY OF THE FITTED kappa^2 MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - every coefficient has the physically correct sign
  - bend-length prefactor B = 1.04 against a first-principles value of 1
  - fitted evanescent decay 0.0082 /nm against 0.0075 /nm computed from
    the independently measured n_eff = 2.3518 (9% apart)
