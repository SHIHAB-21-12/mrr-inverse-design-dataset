PHASE 1.6 SUB-STUDY 4 - COUPLING-STRENGTH BOUNDARY CHECK
====================================================================
Requirement (Phase 1.6 item 4, v1.3 Fix 8): confirm the g/Lc range spans
near-zero to near-critical coupling; record kappa^2 at the range extremes
and the implied Q_L ceiling; reconcile against the 10^3-10^5 target band.
This sub-study is designated the sole authority on that question.

CORRECTION TO THE v1.3 SECTION 2.3 SCOPE NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The v1.3 scope note estimated, analytically, that the realistically
populated band "may be closer to 10^3-10^4", on the grounds that a
g = 350 nm coupler would couple ~2% of power, too strongly to reach
Q_L = 10^5. Measurement shows that estimate was wrong: kappa^2 at
g ~ 345-350 nm is 0.2-0.4%, five to ten times weaker than assumed. The
upper decade IS reached. The scope note itself anticipated this outcome
by naming this sub-study the sole authority; its answer is that the
original 10^3-10^5 target band stands as written.

MODEL ASSUMPTIONS AFFECTING THE HIGH-Q CORNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Weakly coupled samples are loss-limited, so their Q_L is sensitive to
the loss model: bend radiation fit residuals of +/-20-40% (single width,
w = 450 nm) and a literature roughness term (1.3 dB/cm). The Q_L of the
~27 samples above 10^5 carries the largest uncertainty in the dataset.
